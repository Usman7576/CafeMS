Privacy notice

What we collect

When you reserve a table we collect your name, mobile number, the date and time, your party size and any notes. If you ask for a receipt at the till, we collect your mobile number. We never see or store card or wallet details.

Why we use it

Only to manage your booking and send your receipt. We do not sell it or use it for marketing.

How we protect it

Your details are encrypted when stored. Staff see only a masked number unless their role needs the full one, and each time a full number is viewed it is recorded.

Security records

To protect your booking and our systems from misuse, our security log records the network address and browser used for each booking request. It never holds your name, number or notes, and it is kept as a security and accounting record.

How long we keep it

We remove your personal details automatically after 90 days. Encrypted backups can hold them for up to 30 days longer before those backups are deleted. Bill totals stay in our records for tax purposes, with nothing that identifies you.

Your choices

Ask any member of staff to have your details erased, and a manager will arrange it with our administrator.
