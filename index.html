Cafe Management System

Run your cafe from one secure place.

BrewDesk replaces paper order slips and scattered spreadsheets with a single platform for taking orders, billing, tracking stock and seeing daily sales — with access limited to the right staff.

Staff Login See features

Why BrewDesk?

Many small and medium cafes still handle orders, billing and stock on paper. That leads to wrong orders, billing mistakes, wasted stock and records that are easy to tamper with. BrewDesk gives every staff member exactly the tools they need — and nothing more.

📝

Fewer order mistakes

Waiters send orders straight to the kitchen queue — no handwriting to misread.

🧾

Accurate bills

Taxes and totals are calculated automatically, with receipts for every sale.

📦

Less stock waste

Inventory updates as items sell, with alerts before you run out.

📊

Reliable sales data

Daily reports show what sold, when, and how much you earned.

Who uses it

Each role sees only the screens it needs (role-based access, least privilege).

RoleWhat they do in BrewDesk
WaiterTake orders at tables, view table status
Kitchen StaffView order queue, update order status
CashierCreate bills, accept payments, print receipts
ManagerManage inventory, approve discounts/refunds, view sales
Admin / OwnerManage users, roles, menu, prices, audit logs

Built with security in mind

BrewDesk is being developed as a Secure Software Development project.

🔐

Strong authentication

Unique accounts, strong passwords, lockout after repeated failed logins.

👥

Role-based access

Every request checked on the server against the user's role.

🛡️

Protected data

HTTPS everywhere, hashed passwords, no card details stored.

📜

Audit logs

Refunds, price and role changes are logged with who and when.
