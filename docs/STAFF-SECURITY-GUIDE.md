# Staff security guide

**Your account can take payments, see customer details or change prices, so keep it to yourself.** Read this before your first shift. It takes five minutes.

## Your account

- **Use a password you use nowhere else.** At least 12 characters, with upper and lower case letters, a number and a symbol. A few unrelated words joined with a number and a symbol work well. Common words with numbers added, such as `Password123!`, are refused.
- **Never share your password or your authenticator codes**, even with a manager. Nobody from the cafe or IT will ever ask for them.
- **Keep your recovery codes somewhere safe and private**, away from your phone. Each one works once.
- **New phone or lost phone?** Tell an Admin straight away. They will send you a new setup link and your old phone will stop working.

## On a shared device

- **Sign out when you walk away.** The system signs you out after 5 idle minutes, but someone could use your account before then.
- **Check Account > Devices** if something looks wrong. You can sign out any device you do not recognise.

## Watch for tricks

- **Links:** the only link you should use to set a password comes from your Admin, in person or through a channel you trust. If a message pressures you to act now, ask first.
- **Phone calls:** someone claiming to be from IT or the payment company who asks for a code is an attacker.
- **The address bar:** only sign in on the cafe's real web address. Look for it before typing your password.

## Handling customer details

- **Numbers are masked on purpose.** Only look up a full number when the job needs it. Each lookup and search is recorded.
- **Do not copy customer details** into personal phones, notes or chats.
- **If a customer asks for their details to be removed**, tell a manager. An Admin can erase them.

## Money

- **Cash and refunds are recorded against your name.** Everything you do can be traced, which also protects you if something goes wrong.
- **Refunds need a reason and a fresh authenticator code.** Over Rs 5,000, a second person must approve.
- **Never approve something you did not check**, even for a colleague in a hurry.

## If something looks wrong

Tell a manager or Admin **straight away**. You will not be in trouble for reporting a mistake. Examples:

- You clicked a suspicious link or typed your password somewhere odd.
- You were signed out or locked out unexpectedly.
- You see an order, refund or price change you did not make.
- A customer says someone contacted them using details only the cafe holds.

Quick reporting matters: the cafe may have only 72 hours to report a data breach.
